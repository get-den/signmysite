# Add signmysite to a Lovable site

Easiest of all — just ask the agent.

**Option 1 — tell Lovable:**

> Add the signmysite widget: `<script src="https://signmysite.com/w/YOUR_ID.js"></script>` before the closing body tag.

(Get `YOUR_ID` from [signmysite.com](https://signmysite.com) → **Get your widget**.)

**Option 2 — even simpler:** tell it

> Add signmysite to my site — see signmysite.com/skill.md

and it'll handle everything.

Publish. The badge appears bottom-right.
