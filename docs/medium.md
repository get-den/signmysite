# Add Den to Medium

**Medium doesn't allow custom code**, so the floating badge can't be embedded on your
Medium profile or stories. That's a Medium limitation — it doesn't run third-party scripts.

You can still be in the Den graph:

**Link your Den profile (works today)**
- Sign in at [den.com](https://den.com) and set up your profile — you get a public page
  at `den.com/@you` with your follower count and notes.
- Add that link to your Medium **bio** (Settings → Profile information), and in your stories.
- People follow you and leave notes on `den.com/@you` directly.

**Have your own site too?** Put the widget there and link your Medium from it — see the
[catchall guide](catchall.md).
