# Add Den to Substack

**Substack doesn't allow custom code**, so the floating badge can't be embedded on
your Substack. That's a Substack limitation — it strips `<script>` from posts and pages.

You can still be in the Den graph. Two options:

**1. Link your Den profile (works today)**
- Sign in at [den.com](https://den.com), set up your profile — you get a public page at
  `den.com/@you` with your follower count and notes.
- Add that link to **Settings → your profile bio**, or pin a post with it.
- People follow and leave you notes on `den.com/@you` directly.

**2. Embed it on your own domain instead**
- If you also have a personal site (even a one-page site), put the widget there and
  link your Substack from it. See the [catchall guide](catchall.md).

> Want the badge on Substack one day? It'll only be possible if Substack starts
> allowing custom embeds.
